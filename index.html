How much should you eat, and how?

🍽️ How much fruit and veg?

400g+
WHO general guidance: about 5 servings of fruit & veg combined per day
1.5–2 cups
Typical fruit target per day for most adults
2–3 cups
Typical vegetable target per day for most adults
½ plate
Simple visual cue for meals: fill half your plate with fruit & veg

What counts as one serving:

  • 1 medium fruit, like an apple or orange
  • 1 cup raw leafy greens, or about 1/2 cup of most other vegetables
  • 1/2 cup chopped, cooked, or canned fruit or vegetables
  • 1/4 cup dried fruit
  • 1/2 cup cooked beans, lentils, or peas can count toward vegetable totals too

These are general population guidelines, not individual medical advice — needs vary by age, activity level, and health conditions. A doctor or registered dietitian can help tailor this to you.

📏 Growth Tracker

Not a diagnosis. A single height, weight, or BMI number doesn't say whether a child's growth is "healthy" — that takes age- and sex-specific percentile charts tracked over real visits by a pediatrician. This tool just logs your own numbers over time so you have them handy; it deliberately does not label anyone's body as healthy or unhealthy. Entries are kept only for this browsing session and are cleared if you close or reload the page.

BMI shown is a simple weight ÷ height² calculation for your own reference — for children it only means something in context of an age- and sex-adjusted growth chart, which is why no category or verdict is shown here. A pediatrician's growth chart is the right tool for that.
